## Production

**Do you keep stock?**
No. Everything is printed on demand: your piece is produced only after you order it. That is why nothing is ever sold out and no edition sits in a warehouse.

**Who prints it?**
We work with Printify and its network of production facilities. Your order is made at the facility closest to your address.

**How long does production take?**
Between 2 and 7 business days before it ships, depending on the product. The exact window is in your confirmation email. [COMPLETAR: confirmar rango real con el proveedor asignado a tu catálogo]

## Shipping

**Do you ship internationally?**
Yes. We ship almost everywhere. Cost and delivery time are calculated at checkout based on your destination.

**How long does delivery take?**
Production plus transit. The estimated total is shown at checkout before you pay. [COMPLETAR: plazos de tránsito por zona confirmados con el proveedor]

**Can I track my order?**
Yes. When your order leaves the production facility you get an email with the tracking number.

**Who pays customs and import taxes?**
The buyer. Your piece is often printed outside your country, so your destination may charge import duties. Those charges are not included in the product or shipping price.

## Returns

**Can I return it if I change my mind?**
No. Every piece is manufactured specifically for your order, so there are no change-of-mind returns. Check size and variant carefully before paying.

**What if it arrives damaged or misprinted?**
We replace it or refund you. Write to us within [COMPLETAR: plazo de reclamo, ej. 30 días] of delivery, with photos of the product and the packaging.

**I entered the wrong address. What now?**
Tell us as soon as possible. If the order has not entered production we fix it. If it already shipped, we wait for it to come back and reship it with a new shipping charge.

## The products

**What is the poster serial?**
An identifier for the data snapshot that generated that image. The same numbers always generate the same piece; the serial certifies which numbers those were.

**Are editions reprinted?**
No. Each edition is 100 units. When it closes, it closes.

**Are the stickers waterproof?**
Yes: die-cut waterproof vinyl, made for a laptop, a bottle or a helmet.

**Can I order a piece made from my own data?**
Not automated yet, but we do it on request. Write to us.

## It started as a dashboard

HK23 has been logging its own work for years: sessions, messages, active days, streaks. Numbers nobody usually cares about, that usually delete themselves.

One day those numbers got plotted on a grid. The grid looked good. Good enough to frame.

## How a piece is made

The engine takes a real activity snapshot and turns it into an image through a deterministic function:

- the number of filled cells comes from active days,
- the depth of the blue comes from sessions,
- the coral spikes come from the longest streak.

Because the process is pure, the same numbers always return the same image. Nobody else has your numbers, so nobody else can have that image. That is why every edition carries a printed serial: it identifies that snapshot and no other.

## Two collections

**PROOF OF WORK** is the mother collection: your work made object. Stickers as the way in, numbered posters in editions of 100.

**IDLE CYCLES** is the other side. Generative art made to decorate, with no statistics behind it. It is what the machine imagines when it is not working.

## How it reaches you

Everything is printed on demand through Printify. Nothing exists until you order it: no warehouse, no dead stock, and a catalog that can grow without physical limits. Your order is produced at the facility closest to your address and ships from there.

## Who is behind it

## What we collect

**When you buy:** name, email, shipping and billing address, phone if you provide it, order details and transaction data. Full card details are processed by the payment provider; we do not store them.

**When you browse:** IP address, browser and device type, pages viewed and referral source, through cookies and similar technologies.

**When you subscribe or write to us:** your email and the content of your message.

## What we use it for

- Processing, producing and shipping your order.
- Answering questions and handling claims and refunds.
- Fraud prevention and compliance with legal and tax obligations.
- Sending marketing emails, only if you subscribe. You can unsubscribe from any email.
- Understanding what sells and how the store is used, in aggregate.

## Who we share it with

We share the minimum necessary with:

- **Shopify**, which runs the store and the checkout.
- **Printify** and its production facilities, which manufacture and ship your order. This includes your name and shipping address.
- **Carriers**, for delivery and tracking.
- **Payment providers**, to charge you and to prevent fraud.
- **Authorities**, when the law requires it.

We do not sell your personal data.

## International transfers

The store, production and shipping happen outside Chile. When you buy, your data is transferred to the countries where Shopify, Printify and their production facilities operate, which may have different protection standards than your own country.

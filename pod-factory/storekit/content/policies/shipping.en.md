## How your order is made and shipped

HK23 STUDIO is a print-on-demand store. We hold no inventory: every piece is manufactured after you confirm your purchase, through our production partner Printify and its network of print facilities.

Your order is produced at the facility closest to your shipping address and ships from that country, which may not be Chile.

## Destinations

We ship to most countries. If your destination is unavailable at checkout, it is because the production partner does not cover that route.

## Tracking

When your order leaves the production facility we email you a tracking number. Some economy methods only provide tracking up to the destination country's border.

## Customs, duties and import taxes

International orders may be subject to customs duties, tariffs or import taxes in the destination country. **These charges are the buyer's responsibility** and are not included in the product price or the shipping cost. We cannot estimate them in advance, and we cannot mark parcels as gifts or declare a value lower than the real one.

If a parcel is held at customs and the buyer does not pay the duties, the order is treated as refused (see below).

## Incorrect addresses

Check your address before paying. If you tell us before the order enters production, we correct it at no cost. If the order already shipped to an incorrect address provided by the buyer, reshipping carries an additional shipping charge.

## Undelivered, refused or returned parcels

If a parcel comes back for an invalid address, because it was not collected, or because the recipient refused it, we contact you to arrange reshipment with a new shipping charge. With no reply within [COMPLETAR: plazo, ej. 30 días], the order is closed without refunding the production cost.

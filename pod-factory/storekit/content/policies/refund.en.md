## Made-to-order products

All our products are printed on demand: they are manufactured specifically for your order and never return to inventory. For that reason **we do not accept change-of-mind returns or exchanges**, including wrong size or a color picked by mistake.

Check the size, the variant and the preview before paying. If you are unsure, write to us before you buy.

## When we do step in

We replace the piece or refund you, at our discretion, when:

- the product arrives damaged,
- the product arrives with a print defect (color bleed, wrong cut, wrong image),
- you received a different product than the one you ordered,
- the parcel is lost in transit and the carrier confirms it.

## What we do not cover

- Unpaid customs duties, tariffs or import taxes.
- Incorrect or incomplete addresses provided by the buyer.
- Parcels not collected or refused at destination.
- Minor color differences between screen and print. Monitors are not calibrated to each other and every material renders differently.
- Wear from use, sun exposure or bad sticker installation.

## Cancellations

You can cancel while the order has not entered production; in that case the refund is full. Once it enters production it can no longer be cancelled, because the piece is already being made.
